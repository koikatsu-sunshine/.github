## Introduction
**Koikatsu Sunshine** (コイカツ サンシャイン) is a Japanese adult life simulation and dating game developed by **Illusion**, released on August 27, 2021. As the sequel to *Koikatsu Party*, it enhances the original's character customization, interaction mechanics, and open-world exploration while retaining its anime-style aesthetics.

---

## Key Features

### 1. **Advanced Character Creation**
- Utilizes **Illusion's** proprietary "CharaStudio" system.
- Offers extensive sliders for facial features, body proportions, and outfits.
- Supports mods (e.g., *KKManager*, *BetterRepack*) for expanded customization.

### 2. **Open-World Gameplay**
- Explore a tropical island setting with day/night cycles.
- Interact with NPCs through dynamic events and mini-games.
- New "Adventure Mode" allows sandbox-style exploration.

### 3. **Improved AI & Relationships**
- NPCs exhibit more realistic behaviors and routines.
- Dialogue options affect relationship status (friendship, romance, etc.).
- Expanded "H-scene" mechanics compared to *Koikatsu Party*.

### 4. **VR & Modding Support**
- Compatible with **VR headsets** (Oculus Rift, HTC Vive).
- Active modding community provides:
  - Additional clothing/hair assets.
  - Gameplay tweaks (e.g., *BepInEx* plugins).
  - Localization patches for non-Japanese players.
